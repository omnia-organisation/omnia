Connect your Gmail

Omnia reads your 50 latest inbox emails and sorts them into groups it finds by itself.

  • Read-only. Omnia can't send, delete or change anything.
  • Nothing is saved on a server. Your emails load into this tab and are gone when you close it.
  • Sorting runs on your device using simple rules. It isn't AI yet.

Google will warn you that Omnia isn't verified. That's expected while it's private. Tap Advanced, then Go to Omnia (unsafe).
